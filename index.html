It's Me

It's My Journey

I am an educator with a strong focus on science and technology, driven by a passion for developing a generation that is critical, creative, and adaptable to the rapid changes of the modern world. For me, education is not merely about delivering knowledge, but about nurturing curiosity, shaping character, and inspiring students to think beyond the boundaries of the classroom.

As a science teacher and technology enthusiast, I have a deep interest in digital innovation in education. I believe that integrating science, technology, and creativity can create more meaningful and engaging learning experiences. Through the use of tools such as coding, artificial intelligence, and digital media, I strive to design learning environments that are relevant to the era of digital transformation.

Beyond academics, I am also actively involved as a basketball coach, where I emphasize discipline, teamwork, and resilience. I believe that character development is not only built inside the classroom but also through sports, experiences, and real challenges that help shape integrity and leadership in the younger generation.
